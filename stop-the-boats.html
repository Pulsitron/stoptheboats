Field manual

How to Play

Destroy every boat before it reaches the shoreline. Larger craft carry more passengers, need more hits and earn more points. One landing ends the defence.

Controls

MoveA / D or ← / →. On touch, drag the soldier.
FireSpace, click or tap the sea.
CollectMove beneath floating supply buoys.
DeployTap a stored weapon or press 1, 2 or 3.

Power-up key

Field manual

How to Play

Clear every contact to reach the next wave. Chain kills within 3.5 seconds for up to ×1.5 points. Larger craft need more hits. One unblocked landing ends the operation. All upgrades reset on a new run.

Return fire: three direct missile hits end the run. Orange lifeboats warn you with a red sight for one second before firing. The first hit removes one leg: you hop at 72% movement speed, with a smaller hop boost. The second removes the other leg: you crawl at 42% speed and cannot dash. You can still fire, snipe and deploy weapons in either state. Each hit gives 1.5 seconds of recovery so one burst cannot take all three lives. The third hit kills you. Destroying an aiming boat cancels its shot. A boat reaching an undefended shoreline still ends the game immediately.

Sniper: press E or tap SNIPER for a 4× scope with blacked-out surroundings. Click or press Space to fire. On touch screens, drag the view for fine aiming, then hold FIRE with another finger to shoot. Headshots earn +15 before the passengers sink; your running headshot count stays visible below Contact Wave in the top bar. Shots reload in 0.7 seconds. Tap RIFLE or press E to return to automatic rifle fire; the action never pauses.

Audio: open the speaker icon or Audio on the main menu for separate Music and Sound Effects switches. If your browser blocks autoplay, use Play clip.

Conditions: each run has a different weather order, duration, wind and intensity. Nights are dark: sweeping lighthouse beams and your flares reveal parts of the sea. Press F or tap FLARE for about 16 seconds of illumination. Start with three charges; one replenishes every 35 seconds. A new run begins in clear daylight.

Rankings: each of the five boards keeps your personal best for score, boats stopped, best chain, time held or headshots. Military badges use your overall global score position: Recruit is unranked, Private is the lowest posted grade, and the #1 player is Field Marshal. Open the rank ladder for every grade. Share Score uses your last completed run; Share Rank uses your personal records.

Controls

MoveA / D or ← / →. On touch, hold the left or right thumb button. You can also drag the soldier to move and fire together.
FireHold FIRE with your other thumb, Space, or the mouse to keep firing. Movement, fire and support weapons work together. Machine-gun audio follows the standard, upgraded or rapid-fire cadence; sniper mode uses its own recorded rifle report.
CollectMove beneath floating supply buoys.
DeployTap a stored weapon or press 1, 2 or 3.
DashShift or DASH gives a quick burst. One leg allows a smaller hop boost; crawling disables dash.
IlluminationF or FLARE lights the sea. In sniper mode, it illuminates the area you are aiming at.
PauseP or Escape. Help and rankings also pause the action.

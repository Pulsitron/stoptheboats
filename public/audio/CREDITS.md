## Recorded sound effects

The following source pages identify their recordings as **CC0 1.0** and permit copying, modification and redistribution, including commercial use. Licence: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. Sources checked 7 November 2026.

Public playback-preview MP3s published by Freesound were used; no account-restricted original download was accessed. The game embeds local, edited versions rather than hotlinking the recordings.

Adaptations: individual events selected, leading dead air removed where needed, mono downmix for game-controlled spatial placement, low-frequency rumble filtering, short click-prevention fades, static volume normalization and MP3 encoding. The jet recording is a 3.2-second flyby excerpt with a soft entrance and tail. No synthesized sound layers were added. Small, medium and large explosions are mapped to actual recorded grenade/EOD detonations, rather than recordings claiming to match each boat's construction.

Source-preview URLs, exact selection times and measured raw/processed loudness are recorded in `sfx-provenance.json` alongside this file.

The machine-gun and sniper upgrade uses the public high-quality preview MP3s. Each machine-gun asset contains one actual shot, not a burst played on every bullet. Firing cadence is controlled by gameplay; alternate takes and natural tail lengths distinguish the rate profiles. The sniper retains its recorded outdoor echo. No synthesized layers, artificial pitch shifting or time-stretching were added to these assets. Individual-shot volume is peak-limited with static gain before the game's shared mix limiter; the recordings' attack dynamics are preserved.
